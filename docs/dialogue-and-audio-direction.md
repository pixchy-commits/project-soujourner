# Dialogue and audio review pass

Home uses two presentation types, with unchanged script wording:

- Narration: navy panel, cream text, “The story / Narrator” identity, no portrait.
- Character dialogue: rounded light panel, left circular portrait, name and “Transmission” label. Sojo uses cyan, Pathfinder gold, Mission Control pale blue.

Color is redundant with names, portraits and panel shape. This makes speaker recognition possible without relying on hue. Narration has a quieter visual hierarchy; character dialogue foregrounds social identity. Full text reserves its space before the typewriter finishes. Keyboard focus remains visible; clicking or pressing the button reveals text before continuing.

Temporary portraits are replaceable SVGs under `public/art/portraits/`. They are placeholders, not approved character artwork. Edit the voice map in `src/game/narrative/player/Dialogue.tsx` to use new filenames.

## Audio

Seven original synthesized temporary loops are generated by `scripts/generate-scene-score.py`. No third-party music or paid service is involved. These are review beds, not final recorded compositions.

| Phase | Cue |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Gentle invitation |
| Archive / identification | Sparse minor archive bed |
| Entry / landing handoff | Lower pulse and tension |
| Arrival / deployment / friendship / chapter cards | Open, bright wonder |
| Earth flashback after the eye shot | Soft nostalgic melody |
| Mission introduction / exploration / analysis / briefing | Light steady pulse |
| Celebration | Brighter rising melody |

URLs and scene routing live in `src/game/audio/score.ts`. Replace a track there to change music without changing story scripts. Shared phases retain the same track instead of restarting at every shot. Cue changes crossfade over 900ms. Device narration ducks music; hidden tabs pause it. Music remains opt-in via the music button and uses the existing volume settings.

Sojo has a higher chirp, Pathfinder a lower blip, and narration a quiet mechanical tick. Typing sounds are throttled and disabled during device narration. Existing click, transition and CRT rewind cues remain connected.

### Sound design pass

`use-scene-ambience.ts` adds a separate low-level ambient layer, controlled by Sound effects: archive hum, entry rumble, Mars atmosphere, and Earth laboratory hum. These are original synthesized placeholders, including the Mars bed, rather than field recordings. Ambience starts only after interaction, crossfades over 1.2 seconds, pauses in panels/hidden tabs, and ducks during voice and transitions. It stops on return to title.

Chapter cards get a short rising three-note cue. Earth-memory transitions get a descending bell cue; Pathfinder's first reply gets radio connection tones. The scripted celebration gets a short success motif. Normal scene advances use only the quiet button sound, avoiding a repeated transition sweep. Opening settings ducks music. Music and ambience remain independently governed by their existing Music and Sound effects sliders; the corner music toggle affects music only.

All added effects and ambience can be regenerated with `python scripts/generate-game-audio.py`. The score generator is separate so effects revisions do not overwrite music. Asset URLs are plain local WAV paths and can be replaced with final recordings later.

### Distinct timbres and montage synchronization

The active score is now `public/audio/score-v2/`; previous score files are retained. Title uses medium-tempo plucked arpeggios, archive a sparse radio drone without a beat, descent low fast tom pulses, wonder slow sustained chords, memory high music-box partials, exploration dry rapid marimba-like notes, and celebration bright chord stabs. These deliberately differ in rhythm, register and envelope, not just pitch.

ArchiveMontage invokes `montage-in` from its GSAP timeline at each photo's appearance (1.1-second spacing). `montage-shuffle` starts with the grid rearrangement. Both use broadband whooshes rather than the robot's tonal voice patch. Timeline pause and cleanup also govern cue scheduling; reduced-motion mode skips these motion sounds. No separate timeouts run the montage effects.

Kenney Interface Sounds was researched at https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds (listed CC0), but the attempted download returned HTML rather than a ZIP. No third-party audio was imported. The active whooshes and v2 score remain locally synthesized originals. Listening approval is pending.

No build, lint, browser review, or listening review was performed in this pass, as requested. Team review is still needed for portrait sizing, contrast against each shot, music levels, and the temporary sound direction.
